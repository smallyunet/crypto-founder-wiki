## Evidence first

The project separates primary records, direct interviews, reputable secondary reporting, inference, and unverified claims. “No evidence found” describes the boundary of public research; it does not prove mathematical non-existence.

## Stable entities

People, networks, organizations, and reports use stable English IDs. Translated display text does not change their relationships.

## Dated rankings

Network rankings are stored as dated snapshots with a named provider and metric. Market cap, TVL, and activity are not merged into an unexplained composite score.

## Bilingual publication

The first three imported dossiers originated in Simplified Chinese; later dossiers may be researched in both languages together. Every edition preserves paths, entity IDs, source attribution, and evidence strength.

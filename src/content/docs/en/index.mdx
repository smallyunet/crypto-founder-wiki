## Designed for expansion

- **Stable entity IDs:** people, networks, and articles remain linked across languages without relying on display names.
- **Dated ranking snapshots:** market cap, TVL, and activity rankings can be preserved without overwriting history.
- **Bilingual coverage checks:** every covered person and network must include both Chinese and English pages.
- **Evidence first:** confirmed facts, reasonable inferences, disputed claims, and unverified stories are labeled separately.

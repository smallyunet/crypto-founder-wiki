## How formal rankings enter the repository

Every ranking snapshot must preserve:

1. the data date and retrieval date;
2. the provider and original source link;
3. the ranking method, such as market capitalization, TVL, or active addresses;
4. network identifiers and their values at that time; and
5. missing data, mapping disputes, and manual corrections.

Formal snapshots never overwrite earlier files. This preserves what a given methodology showed on a particular date and prevents time-sensitive network rankings from being hard-coded into person profiles.

:::note[Finding]
Gavin Wood was one of Ethereum's early co-founders, wrote the Yellow Paper, and led the first production-grade Ethereum client. His later Parity and Polkadot roles created additional economic exposure, but project treasuries and token market caps are not his personal assets.
:::

## Technical formation and Ethereum

Wood trained in computing and completed doctoral work before joining Ethereum's early team. He formalized the protocol in the Yellow Paper, implemented the C++ client, and served as the Ethereum Foundation's first chief technology officer. These contributions make “co-founder” accurate while also showing that Ethereum was a multi-founder project. [Ethereum history](https://ethereum.org/en/history/) [Yellow Paper](https://ethereum.github.io/yellowpaper/paper.pdf)

## Parity, Web3 Foundation, and Polkadot

After Ethereum, Wood co-founded Ethcore, renamed Parity Technologies, and proposed Polkadot. Web3 Foundation commissioned and stewards parts of the ecosystem. His wealth path plausibly includes founder interests, compensation, and token exposure across these ventures, but no complete public cap table or wallet-and-liability schedule establishes a precise net worth. [Gavin Wood biography](https://gavwood.com/) [Polkadot white paper](https://wiki.polkadot.com/general/getting-started/)

## Assessment

Wood's protocol authorship and co-founder status are primary-source supported. Personal allocations, realized sales, taxes, and present ownership remain only partially observable.

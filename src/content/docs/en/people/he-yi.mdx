## Career and operating capital

Public profiles place He in television presentation and marketing before she helped build OKCoin and later worked at mobile-video company Yixia Technology. Those roles supplied audience-growth, brand, recruiting, and fundraising networks rather than evidence of inherited crypto capital. In 2017 she joined Changpeng Zhao in building Binance and became central to branding, user growth, and later investment operations. [Binance leadership](https://www.binance.com/en/blog/leadership) [Fortune profile](https://fortune.com/2022/07/18/binance-female-cofounder-he-yi-cz-crypto-blockchain/)

## Capital path and ownership limits

Her plausible wealth channels are founder equity, BNB-related economic interests, compensation or distributions, and investment-management interests. These are not interchangeable with Binance customer deposits, exchange trading volume, company treasury, or the assets managed by Binance Labs/YZi Labs. No public cap table or complete beneficial-ownership filing supports a precise percentage.

## Assessment

He should be described as a co-founder and operating executive, not merely a marketing employee. Her role is well supported; the size and liquidity of her personal wealth remain unverified.

## Career and operating capital

Public profiles place He in television presentation and marketing before she helped build OKCoin and later worked at mobile-video company Yixia Technology. Those roles supplied audience-growth, brand, recruiting, and fundraising networks rather than evidence of inherited crypto capital. In 2017 she joined Changpeng Zhao in building Binance and became central to branding, user growth, and later investment operations. [Binance leadership](https://www.binance.com/en/blog/leadership) [Fortune profile](https://fortune.com/2022/07/18/binance-female-cofounder-he-yi-cz-crypto-blockchain/)

## Family relationship with CZ

Yi He and Changpeng Zhao (CZ) share both a Binance co-founding history and a family relationship as parents. A **January 24, 2025** Bloomberg News report hosted by Bloomberg Tax explicitly states that they **have three children together**. This is a documented biographical fact, more specific than describing them only as close business associates. [Bloomberg on YZi Labs and the founders' wealth](https://news.bloombergtax.com/financial-accounting/cryptos-richest-man-cz-turns-vc-firm-into-giant-family-office)

The report supports shared parenthood and the number of children, but does not establish a registered marriage. This dossier therefore does not label He as CZ's wife. Parenthood also does not establish ownership percentages, joint-property arrangements, or inheritance rights.

## Executive and investment timeline

- **August 3, 2022**: Binance appointed He to lead Binance Labs, adding a formal venture-investment and incubation role. [Binance appointment release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/co-founder-yi-he-to-lead-binances-7-5b-venture-capital-arm-301598732.html)
- **Early 2025**: Binance Labs became YZi Labs. In a **February 2** public post, He said it operated independently, with Ella Zhang as head and CZ directly managing Labs. She described Labs investments and Binance listings as decisions of separate teams. This is her account of the organization, rather than independent verification of every conflict-of-interest control. [He's explanation of Labs](https://www.binance.com/zh-CN/square/post/19764424467298)
- **December 3, 2025**: Binance named He co-CEO alongside Richard Teng. The announcement highlighted her community, user-growth, and product-innovation responsibilities. [Binance co-CEO announcement](https://www.binance.com/en/blog/leadership/4951765043144430728)

Her 2022 Labs appointment must be distinguished from the subsequent YZi Labs management structure; the historical title does not establish that she still directly leads YZi Labs.

## Capital path and ownership limits

Her plausible wealth channels are founder equity, BNB-related economic interests, compensation or distributions, and investment-management interests. These are not interchangeable with Binance customer deposits, exchange trading volume, company treasury, or the assets managed by Binance Labs/YZi Labs. No public cap table or complete beneficial-ownership filing supports a precise percentage.

The Bloomberg report also said YZi Labs would manage wealth for both Zhao and He. That documents a connection between the family and an investment platform, but does not disclose her individual beneficial share. Assets under management are not her personal net worth. [Bloomberg report](https://news.bloombergtax.com/financial-accounting/cryptos-richest-man-cz-turns-vc-firm-into-giant-family-office)

## Assessment

A rounded dossier covers her co-founder and co-CEO responsibilities, the public report of three children with CZ, and her investment-management history. Both her family relationship and professional contribution are documented; the size and liquidity of her personal wealth remain unverified.

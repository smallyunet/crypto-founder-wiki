## Background and operating capital

Gokal studied finance at Wharton and worked in investing, then health technology. Public biographies list General Catalyst, Rock Health, Sano, and Omada Health. This supplied startup finance, product, fundraising, and regulated-industry experience before Solana. [Personal site](https://rajgokal.com/) [SXSW biography](https://schedule.sxsw.com/2025/contributors/2057274)

## Solana role

Gokal joined the founding team in 2017 and became the principal public counterpart to Yakovenko on operations and ecosystem growth. The distinction matters: Yakovenko proposed the clock and architecture; engineers built clients; Gokal helped turn the technical project into a financed organization and developer ecosystem.

## Wealth boundary

Founding status makes company equity and team-token allocation plausible, but precise percentages, vesting, sales, tax and liabilities are private. Capital raised by Solana Labs, Foundation grants, ecosystem TVL, and total SOL market capitalization cannot be counted as Gokal's assets.

## Assessment

Gokal is properly a company and ecosystem co-founder, not the sole protocol inventor. His wealth path is likely equity and token based, but no auditable current number is available.
